About Chapter One

Chapter One is a small, independent bookshop for readers who love a quiet corner and a good story.

We curate a compact catalogue of fiction, mystery, science fiction and history — handpicked titles rather than endless shelves. Every book is chosen because someone here couldn't stop talking about it.

This storefront is a teaching demo for the AI-Augmented SDLC course, so orders aren't real — but the reading recommendations are.
